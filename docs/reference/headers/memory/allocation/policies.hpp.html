Type reference

micron::allocation_policy

Defined in header <micron/memory/allocation/policies.hpp>.

Compile-time parameters for initial byte grants, grant granularity, and rational growth. Granularity and GrowthDenominator must be nonzero, and the growth numerator must be at least the denominator.

Policy types

minimum_bytes, granularity, growth_numerator, and growth_denominator are the canonical integer parameters. on_grow is a floating-point compatibility value. All provided policies declare concurrent == false; shareable is false except in the constrained policy.

InterfaceDescription
serial_allocation_policyMinimum and granularity page_size; growth 3/1.
small_allocation_policyMinimum and granularity 512 bytes; growth 2/1.
constrained_allocation_policyMinimum and granularity 256 bytes; growth 3/2; shareable == true.
huge_allocation_policyMinimum and granularity large_page_size (2 MiB); growth 4/1.
exact_allocation_policyMinimum zero, granularity one byte, growth 1/1.

Notes

Policies describe rounding and growth; they do not select an operating-system mapping, add locks, construct objects, or implement shared ownership. is_policy checks the presence of the required named fields, rather than proving that an arbitrary custom policy satisfies the arithmetic invariants.
