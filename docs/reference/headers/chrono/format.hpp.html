micron::chrono::offset_style

Defined in header <micron/chrono/format.hpp>.

Formatting policy for omitting an offset, selecting Z at zero, or emitting signed compact/colon notation.

Related policy

date_sep selects ISO T, space, or the currently equivalent none path. Offset styles are none, z_or_colon, colon, compact, and z_or_compact. These values control spelling only; they do not convert calendar fields or load zone data.

Related types and selection guide.
