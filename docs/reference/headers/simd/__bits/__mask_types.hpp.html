micron::simd::__bits::__mmask8

Defined in header <micron/simd/__bits/__mask_types.hpp>.

Unsigned scalar mask carrier with 8 low-order lane-selection bits.

Mask family

AliasUnderlying typeBit capacity
__mmask8unsigned char8
__mmask16unsigned short16
__mmask32unsigned int32
__mmask64unsigned long long64

Helpers and use

mask_lanes_v<M> is sizeof(M)*8, and k_mask_type accepts the four exact unsigned aliases. Bit i selects lane i in the relevant mask operation; unused high bits do not enlarge the register. These are plain scalar integers, not dynamically sized Boolean vectors. Vendor-style global aliases are injected when the type-injection macro is defined. Packet comparison integers have their separate backend-specific encodings and cannot always be used as one-bit-per-lane masks.
