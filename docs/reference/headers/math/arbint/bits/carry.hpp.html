micron::math::mpn::acc3

Defined in header <micron/math/arbint/bits/carry.hpp>.

A three-limb unsigned accumulator for column-oriented multiplication.

Members and cost

Public c0,c1,c2 contain the low word, next word, and column overflow count. fma adds a word product, fma2 adds it twice, add adds one word, and shift_out emits c0 then shifts the other two words down. Each operation is O(1), allocating nothing. The caller must keep a column's product count within the three-word accumulator's range.
