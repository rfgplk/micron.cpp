micron::math::mpn::limb_t

Defined in header <micron/math/arbint/limb.hpp>.

The unsigned native-width storage word used by the multi-precision limb layer.

Word aliases and constants

limb_t/slimb_t are u64/i64 on 64-bit targets and u32/i32 otherwise; dlimb_t is exactly twice the limb width (uint128_t or u64). limb_bits, limb_bytes, limb_max, and limb_msb describe them. limbs_for(bits) rounds upward, and limbs_of<Bits> is its compile-time form. Arithmetic size products must fit usize.

lo_half, hi_half, join, leading/trailing-zero, bit-length, and population-count helpers operate on one word in O(1). Zero leading/trailing counts return limb_bits. These aliases carry no allocation, sign convention for the whole integer, or normalization policy.
