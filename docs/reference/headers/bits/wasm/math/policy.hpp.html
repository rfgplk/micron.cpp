Type reference

micron::math::policy::extended_tag

Defined in header <micron/bits/wasm/math/policy.hpp>.

Empty policy selecting the binary128 transcendental backend without a global faithful-rounding guarantee.

Policy selection

extended is the inline constant policy value. default_tag<F> selects extended_tag for a 16-byte F and faithful_tag otherwise. The validation helper rejects a different policy for binary128 transcendental evaluation. This restriction concerns the implementation's error guarantee, not the representation or availability of basic binary128 arithmetic.
